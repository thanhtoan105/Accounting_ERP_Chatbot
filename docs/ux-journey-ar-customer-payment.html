3) ValidationRequired Fields • Amount Rules • Period Open
  • Required: Date, Customer, Bank/Cash account, at least one applied amount.
  • Apply amount ≤ Invoice Due; total applied ≤ Receipt amount (if receipt amount specified).
  • Period open; accounts 111/112 and 131 are postable.
  • Overpayment handling:
    • If Total Applied < Receipt Amount → difference becomes Customer Credit (advance) on 131.
    • If Total Applied > Receipt Amount → show inline error and prevent posting.
On success, status = Posted; affected invoice updated to Paid/Partially Paid.
4) PostingDouble‑entry
  • Dr 111/112 (Cash/Bank) = Receipt Amount
  • Cr 131 (Customer) = Total Applied (by customer_id)
  • Cr 131 (Customer, advance) = Overpayment (if any) — tracked as credit balance to offset future invoices
