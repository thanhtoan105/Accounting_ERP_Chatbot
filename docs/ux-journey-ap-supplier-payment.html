3) ValidationRequired Fields • Amount Rules • Period Open
  • Required: Date, Supplier, Bank/Cash account, at least one pay amount.
  • Pay amount ≤ Bill Due; total applied ≤ Payment amount (if payment amount specified).
  • Period open; accounts 111/112 and 331 are postable.
  • Payment amount > 0 — negative payments are not allowed.
  • Bank balance check (warning only): if Payment Amount > selected bank balance → show non‑blocking warning.
  • Duplicate payment check (warning): same supplier + same bills paid within last X days.
  • Payment terms check: if paying before bill due date and terms have early-payment discount → suggest/auto‑apply discount line (optional confirmation).
  • Prepayment handling:
    • If Total Applied < Payment Amount → difference recorded as Prepayment/Advance to supplier on 331 (debit balance).
    • If Total Applied > Payment Amount → inline error; prevent posting.
4) PostingDouble‑entry
  • Dr 331 (Supplier) = Total Applied (by supplier_id)
  • Cr 111/112 (Cash/Bank) = Payment Amount
  • Dr 331 (Supplier, advance) = Prepayment amount (if any)
Status updates: Bills to Paid/Partially Paid; prepayment available to apply on future bills.
