# Product Vision

## What We're Building

**Vietnam TT200-Compliant Accounting ERP** - A compliance-first accounting platform purpose-built for Vietnamese SMEs, chief accountants, CFOs, and accounting students.

## Target Users

### Primary: Chief Accountants & Finance Leaders
- Vietnamese SMEs (20-300 employees)
- Responsible for TT200 compliance, month-end close, statutory reporting (B01-DN, B02-DN, B03-DN)
- Pain: slow closes (10-15 days), frequent posting errors, audit stress, high consulting costs

### Secondary: Accounting Students & Juniors
- Early-career professionals or university students
- Need TT200-compliant workflows for training and rapid onboarding
- Pain: steep learning curve, no embedded regulatory help

## Core Value Proposition

1. **Preconfigured TT200 COA & Policy Guardrails** - Out-of-the-box account structure, leaf-only GL posting rules
2. **Field-Level Compliance Validation** - Real-time checks ensure only TT200-compliant postings reach the ledger
3. **AI-Powered RAG Chatbot** - Context-aware guidance with article citations, >80% self-serve rate
4. **Immutable Audit Trails** - Every action logged, double-entry enforced

## Success Metrics

- 30-40% reduction in month-end close time (from 10-15 days to 5-7 days)
- 50%+ reduction in posting errors
- >80% regulatory queries resolved via in-app chatbot
- NPS >8/10 on compliance confidence

## MVP Scope (In)

- TT200-compliant Chart of Accounts with leaf-only posting rules
- Core operations: AR invoicing, AP bills, General Ledger
- TT200 financial statements: B01-DN, B02-DN, B03-DN with drill-down
- VAT handling (0/5/10/exempt) with automatic GL mapping
- AI RAG chatbot using Pinecone + Azure OpenAI
- Single-currency (VND) only

## Out of Scope (MVP)

- Deep e-invoice integration
- Multi-currency / IFRS
- Custom workflow engine
- Mobile-native app
- Predictive analytics
